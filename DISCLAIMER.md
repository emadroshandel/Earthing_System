# Disclaimer

EarthSystem is an engineering aid, not an authority.

Every result it produces is conditional on the inputs it was given: the soil
model, the fault current, the clearing time, the geometry and the material
data. A "PASS" badge is a statement about a model, not about a site.

Before anything computed here is built:

* the soil model must be verified by measurement, in the worst season;
* the fault current and clearing time must come from a protection study, not
  from an assumption;
* the design must be reviewed and approved by an engineer competent in the
  applicable standards and qualified in the relevant jurisdiction;
* the installed earthing system must be tested after construction, and
  periodically thereafter.
