Numerical analysis — boundary-element solver

Arbitrary electrode geometry in uniform or two-layer soil. The buried metal is discretised into segments held at a common potential; solving for the leakage-current distribution gives the true earth resistance, the surface potential field and the touch and step voltages everywhere — without the shape restrictions of the closed-form equations.
